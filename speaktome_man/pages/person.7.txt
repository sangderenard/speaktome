NAME
       person - an agent, and the five ways the system knows one

DESCRIPTION
       An agent is anyone who acts: a human, a model or a script. The
       system never holds an agent whole. It holds facets, each a record
       in a different place.

           account      The agent's standing on one server: a login and
                        what it may do.
           principal    The account as one request sees it, from where
                        the request came.
           entity       The agent in a world: a cursor on a graph.
           author       Who wrote a thing, as the repository's history
                        records it.
           contact      Another agent this one has agreed to hear from.

   The one authority
       The accounts of the service are the only real users. Every other
       record that names a person is history or description. Nothing
       reads identity from anywhere else.

   What is joined
       The account is the hub. Roles, messages, cursors, workspaces,
       leases and jobs are all keyed to it. A principal is made from the
       account for each request. Contacts are pairs of accounts.

       The account is not yet joined to the entity in a game, nor to the
       author in a repository. A commit names its author; no record says
       which account that is.

   Reserved words
       operator     A role. The human at the machine is the owner.
       session      An account's login. A cursor is a cursor.
       player       The entity an agent controls in a game.
       guest        No account.
       DM           The narrator of a story. A message between contacts
                    is a message, never a DM.
