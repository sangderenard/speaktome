NAME
       take - occupy a place, or hold a resource

SYNOPSIS
       None yet.

DESCRIPTION
       A place nobody has taken is a ruin: a default interior produced on
       demand, which costs nothing and is always available. To take a
       place is to put your own version of it there.

       Several accounts may take the same place. Each keeps its own
       version, and a dial at the door selects among them. Ruins remain
       one of the dial's settings.

       An author of the thing a place stands for may make their version
       the permanent one. A guest keeps a taken place in their own
       browser and is told to download it.

       For a program, take is how a thread of work holds a resource.

IN A STORY
       "This place is mine."

NEEDS
       Not decided.

STATUS
       Planned. There is no endpoint and no tool. The record it will
       write is the occupant entry of a place's document.
