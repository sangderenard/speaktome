NAME
       show - have a picture made of the scene

SYNOPSIS
       Tool:   show [photo={...}] [settings={...}]
               show picture=ID [image=true]
       REST:   POST /api/show/pictures {request_id, settings?, photo?}
               GET  /api/show/pictures/{id}
               GET  /api/marble_photo?digest=DIGEST
               GET  /api/marble_photo/image?digest=DIGEST&name=NAME

DESCRIPTION
       show is look with a camera. Where look answers with the places
       around you, show answers with a picture.

       The picture is made by the Studio's own photo service. A request
       becomes a Studio scene, travels over the system's Nodus contract
       port to the Studio destination, and is committed there as a photo
       job owned by your account. No second renderer or queue is
       involved, and the job appears with your other photo jobs.

       A request may carry two things. The settings are a complete
       Studio settings record: the object, the fully resolved Exposure
       lighting profile, and the camera, whose positions are in metres.
       A settings record replaces the host's baked default record whole;
       the two are never merged. The photo options are the photo
       service's own, such as resolution and seconds. Both may be left
       out when the host has baked defaults.

       Asking is answered at once with a receipt. The receipt's state
       moves from queued to preparing and then ends as one of:

           committed    A photo job exists. The receipt names it.
           refused      Authority was withdrawn; no job was made.
           failed       The scene could not be prepared, or no result
                        came back; no job was made.
           uncertain    The commit raised an error. A job may or may
                        not exist. Nothing is retried.

       A committed picture is not yet a finished one. The exposure runs
       on the one GPU and is followed on the photo endpoints, which also
       serve the image and cancel a job. With a picture id the tool
       reads the receipt and the exposure together, and with image=true
       it attaches the finished image.

       The request carries no identity. The account, its ownership of
       the job and its authority come from the signed-in session, and
       authority is checked again just before the job is made. The hash
       of the prepared scene checks that it arrived whole; it grants
       nothing.

       The request carries a request_id. Repeating it with the same
       content returns the first receipt, marked replayed.

IN A STORY
       "Paint me the scene."

NEEDS
       The photo_submit capability and a signed-in account. Reading the
       exposure and the image needs photo_read.

ANSWER
       picture_id   The receipt's id.
       state        As above.
       job          The photo service's own record of the job, once
                    committed. Its digest names the job.
       status_url   Where the exposure is followed.
       exposure     The exposure's state, size and image name (tool,
                    with a picture id).

REFUSALS
       401  sign in to ask for a picture
       409  request_id already used with different content
       429  picture queue is full; try again when one finishes
                 One picture is prepared at a time and four may wait.
       503  no Studio destination is configured on this host
       404  picture not found
                 The id is unknown or belongs to another account.

EXAMPLE
       show photo={"resolution": 256, "seconds": 5}
           A picture was asked for; it is queued.
       show picture=4f0c... image=true
           The photo job exists and its exposure is complete.

STATUS
       Exists. First live picture: 2026-11-11, 256 pixels, 5 seconds.

NOTES
       The narrator's render-phong command is a different picture: a
       browser page attached to a registered world draws it. It is
       reached at POST /api/living-data/dm/worlds/{world}/commands and
       no tool offers it.

       A picture taken from where a cursor stands does not exist yet.
       The Studio scene is not a place on a map.
