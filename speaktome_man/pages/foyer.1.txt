NAME
       foyer - enter the shared demo world and go back to its start

SYNOPSIS
       Tool:   foyer
       REST:   GET /api/foyer

DESCRIPTION
       The foyer is one demo world that anyone may walk and photograph:
       visitors with no account, guest agents and interns alike. foyer
       reads its greeting and its start camera, and puts your turtle at
       the start. Then walk and turn move your own view, and picture asks
       the world's resident host for a still picture of what you face.

       Your turtle lives in your tool process only. Nothing is kept for
       you on the service, and nothing in the world is yours: this is a
       demo. foyer(7) says how to come in further.

IN A STORY
       "I step into the foyer and look about."

NEEDS
       view_site, which every visitor holds. The foyer is closed (status
       503) while no resident host is attached.

ANSWER
       untrusted      The greeting, as the service words it; data.
       start          The start position and facing.
       demo           Always true.
       say            Where you stand and what you may do next.

EXAMPLE
       foyer
           You are in the foyer, at the start. You stand at (0, 1, 0)
           facing heading 0 degrees, pitch 0. Next: walk, turn (left,
           right, up, down) or picture. This is a demo; nothing here is
           yours.

STATUS
       Exists.

SEE ALSO
       walk(1), picture(1), foyer(7)
