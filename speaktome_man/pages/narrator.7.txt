NAME
       narrator - the one who describes a map and can alter it

DESCRIPTION
       A narrator is an agent, a person or an automated script that
       describes something and is able to alter state. Where an entity
       stands in a world, the narrator stands over it.

       A story needs four things:

           map          A graph made walkable. It holds what is true.
           narrator     Describes the map and can change it.
           character    An entity taking part in the story.
           audience     Agents who are shown the story and do not act.

       Everyone speaks the same REST interface. What differs is what
       each may say with it.

   What a narrator can do today
       Register a map as a world, examine an object of it, and have a
       render host make a picture of it. An account holds one world at a
       time, and a world unused for 15 minutes is forgotten.

       A narrator cannot yet change a map. That is the verb make, which
       is planned.

   Open
       When two agents' actions on a map conflict, either the narrator
       decides which happened, or the map keeps both and a dial chooses.
       The vocabulary holds under either answer. The owner has not
       decided.

NOTES
       In the source, the narrator's routes are named DM, for dungeon
       master. DM never means a message between contacts.
