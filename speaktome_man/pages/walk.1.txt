NAME
       walk, turn - move your own view through the foyer's demo world

SYNOPSIS
       Tool:   walk {metres?}
       Tool:   turn {direction, degrees?}

DESCRIPTION
       Your view in the foyer is a turtle, as in the old Logo turtles: a
       position and a direction. walk carries it forward by whole metres
       (negative walks back), level with the ground. turn turns it left or
       right about the vertical, or tilts it up or down, by whole degrees;
       the tilt stops at 80 degrees either way.

       The world is right-handed with Y up. At heading 0 you face +Z, and
       turning right turns you toward -X.

       Neither verb talks to the service: they move only the view your
       tool process keeps. Nothing in the world moves, and the next
       picture is taken from wherever you then stand. foyer returns you to
       the start.

IN A STORY
       "I walk three paces and turn to my left."

NEEDS
       Nothing. The first walk or turn enters the foyer when you have not.

ANSWER
       position       Where you stand, [x, y, z] in metres.
       facing         The direction you face, a unit vector.
       demo           Always true.
       say            Where you stand and what you may do next.

EXAMPLE
       walk metres=3
           You walk 3 m. You stand at (0, 1, 3) facing heading 0
           degrees, pitch 0. Next: walk, turn (left, right, up, down) or
           picture. This is a demo; nothing here is yours.
       turn direction=left degrees=90

STATUS
       Exists.

SEE ALSO
       foyer(1), picture(1), foyer(7)
