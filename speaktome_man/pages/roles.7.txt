NAME
       roles, capabilities - what an account may do, and why

DESCRIPTION
       The server decides what each request may do. It grants a set of
       capabilities, and each verb needs one of them.

       The capabilities come from two sources, added together: the role
       of the account, and where the request came from. A request from
       the local network is granted more than one from outside, with or
       without a sign-in.

   Roles
           guest        No account. From outside, a guest sees the
                        public site only.
           member       An account with no role given. May write in the
                        studio, submit photos and compiles, share, and
                        message.
           service      An account a program runs under. A member's
                        powers, with library management and federation.
           operator     An account that runs the deployment. A member's
                        powers, with deploy scripts, lifecycle control
                        and federation.
           admin        The account that governs accounts. Everything,
                        including managing accounts and reading any
                        thread.

   Capabilities the verbs need
           dev_ui            enter, where, go, look, leave, examine,
                             show, and the system verbs.
           message           say, pending, acknowledge, contacts.
           account_manage    diagnostics; also reading other accounts'
                             threads. Held only by the admin role.
           lifecycle_admin   diagnostics. Held only by a request from
                             the local network.

       whoami and man need nothing.

       The full list of capability names is in the service's authz
       module, which is the one place they are computed.

   Refusal
       A request without the capability is refused by the server with
       status 403. A verb that also needs an account answers 401 when
       the capability is held but nobody is signed in. A client cannot
       grant itself anything: the tools add no permissions to the
       account they sign in as.
