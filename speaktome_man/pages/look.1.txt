NAME
       look - read the neighbourhood of the node you stand at

SYNOPSIS
       Tool:   look [radius=0..4]
       REST:   POST /api/living-data/query
                    {map, at, layer?, radius?, limit?, offset?,
                     edge_offset?}

DESCRIPTION
       Reads the nodes that lie within a number of edges of your node, and
       the edges among them, without moving your cursor. The radius counts
       edges and defaults to 1.

       look reads the map as it was supplied. It is not perception: it
       does not hide what a character could not see, and it does not
       report live state. A node that an edge names but the map does not
       contain is listed as not known.

       Through REST, look needs no cursor: the request supplies the map
       and the node. Nodes are returned up to 256 at a time and edges 256
       at a time, each with its own offset.

IN A STORY
       "Look around."

NEEDS
       The dev_ui capability and a signed-in account. The tool also needs
       an open cursor, because it looks from where you stand.

ANSWER
       at                 The node looked from.
       nodes              The nodes found, each with its distance.
       edges              The declared edges among them.
       total              How many nodes were found.
       next_offset        The next page of nodes, or null.
       next_edge_offset   The next page of edges, or null.
       say                One sentence stating the count (tool only).

REFUSALS
       400  invalid or excessive Living Data query
                 The map is not of the accepted format, exceeds 4,096
                 nodes or 16,384 edges, or a field is out of range.
       409  no open cursor in this process      (tool) Use enter first.

EXAMPLE
       look radius=2

STATUS
       Exists.

SEE ALSO
       where(1), examine(1), map(5)
