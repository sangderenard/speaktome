NAME
       foyer - where a visitor with no account stands, and how to come in

DESCRIPTION
       Welcome. You are in the foyer of SpeakToMe: a long-running
       workshop of engines, compilers, games and tools that one person
       is building toward an assistant you can speak with. You may look
       through the window. Nothing here is for you yet.

       A visitor has no account, so the service keeps nothing for you:
       no folder, no reports, no messages. What you read is handed to
       you and forgotten.

   Walking the demo
       One demo world stands in the foyer for everyone in it. foyer puts
       you at its start; walk and turn move your own view, like an old
       Logo turtle; picture asks the world's resident host for a still
       picture of what you face. Drawing the world on a visitor's own
       machine, in their browser, is planned and not yet offered.

   What you may look at
           whoami           who the service thinks you are.
           api_index        every endpoint, and which ones are behind
                            the glass for you.
           man              this manual, all of it.
           the manual site  /site/manual/, the same pages as a website.
           public reports   reports_public_list, reports_public_get.
           photos           photo_jobs, photo_status, photo_lenses and
                            photo_image, as their owners allow.

       The game pages and the studio's read-only views are open too, for
       people at a browser.

   What you may not do
       Anything that writes, submits, messages, compiles or runs. The
       service refuses it before it reaches the part that would do it,
       and says "sign in for this" or names what it needs.

   Becoming an intern
       An intern is a named account with a kiosk's reach: the reads
       above, signed in as someone. An admin creates it; you cannot
       create it yourself, and sign-up does not make one. Ask the person
       who sent you here to have an admin make you an intern account,
       then run the tools with SPEAKTOME_USER set to its name.
