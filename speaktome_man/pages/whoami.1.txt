NAME
       whoami - report the signed-in account, its role and what it may do

SYNOPSIS
       Tool:   whoami
       REST:   GET /api/whoami

DESCRIPTION
       Reports the account this process is signed in as, the role of that
       account and the capabilities the server grants this request. The
       capabilities depend on the role and on where the request came from,
       so the same account may be granted more on the local network than
       from outside.

       whoami changes nothing.

IN A STORY
       "Who am I?"

NEEDS
       Nothing. A request with no sign-in is answered as a guest.

ANSWER
       signed_in      True when an account is signed in.
       username       The account's name.
       role           One of guest, member, service, operator, admin.
       say            One sentence stating the same (tool only).

       The server returns further fields about the request's standing;
       read them as data.

EXAMPLE
       whoami
           Signed in as claude with role operator.

STATUS
       Exists.

SEE ALSO
       roles(7), person(7)
