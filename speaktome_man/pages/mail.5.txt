NAME
       mail - mailboxes for the accounts, and mail in and out

DESCRIPTION
       Every person account has a mailbox at

           <username>@stichka.duckdns.org

       Service accounts have none. The mailbox is reached through the
       site, signed in with the account: mail_inbox lists it,
       mail_message reads one item, mail_send sends, mail_remove takes
       an item out. There is no separate mail password.

       Mail comes in on an odd port, 47625, never port 25. Only our own
       servers, or someone who set the port by hand, know there is mail
       to connect to; that is itself a filter. Ordinary mail servers on
       the internet try port 25 only, so their mail does not arrive.

       Mail goes out through a mail provider, from the provider's own
       verified address, shown as "<name> via stichka.duckdns.org". It
       carries no reply address at this domain: the text says that an
       answer goes through the site. Mail to an address outside the
       domain needs the capability mail_send. Mail between accounts of
       the site does not.

   The email manager table
       Each message and each recipient is a row. The rows are routed by
       a table of the Nodus canvas, named mail, as the cron tool's
       tasks are. Whoever stores a row asks the table for one task;
       the mail service, the host of the table, performs it:

           mail.file       Puts the waiting local recipients of one
                           message into their mailboxes.
           mail.send       Hands the waiting outside recipients of one
                           message to the provider.
           mail.sweep      Does both for every row still waiting. The
                           table's own cron tool asks for it every five
                           minutes.

       A request names a message and nothing else: no text, no address
       and no secret.

   States
       A recipient is queued, then one of:

           delivered       In a local mailbox.
           sent            Accepted by the provider.
           deferred        A temporary failure. It is tried again
                           after 5, 15, 60, 240 and 720 minutes, and
                           given up three days after it was sent.
           failed          Refused, or given up.

       Reading a message you sent shows each recipient's state and what
       the provider answered.

   Limits
       10 MB a message, 20 recipients, 20 messages a minute from one
       address, 200 MB a mailbox, 50 outgoing messages a day. Mail is
       never relayed: only addresses of this domain are accepted.
