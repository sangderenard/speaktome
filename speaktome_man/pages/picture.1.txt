NAME
       picture - take one still picture from where you stand in the foyer

SYNOPSIS
       Tool:   picture {width?, height?}
       REST:   POST /api/foyer/commands {request_id, operation:
                 "render-phong", position, facing, width?, height?}
               GET  /api/foyer/commands/<request_id>

DESCRIPTION
       Asks the foyer's resident host to render the demo world from your
       turtle's position and facing, waits for it, and attaches the image.
       The host is a real browser page running the world's own Phong
       renderer; the picture is an actual render, not a stand-in.

       You may have one picture in hand at a time, and the foyer takes at
       most eight at once across everyone in it; a busy foyer answers 429
       and you may ask again. Pictures are at most 640 pixels a side.

       The service keeps your receipt for a short while so you can read it
       again; it keeps nothing else for you.

IN A STORY
       "I raise the camera and take a picture."

NEEDS
       view_site, which every visitor holds, and an attached resident
       host.

ANSWER
       state          complete, queued, rendering or failed.
       camera         The position and facing the picture was asked from.
       png_sha256     The picture's digest, when complete.
       demo           Always true.
       say            What happened and what you may do next.

       A complete picture arrives as an attached PNG image.

EXAMPLE
       picture width=320 height=240
           The picture is attached. You stand at (0, 1, 3) facing
           heading 0 degrees, pitch 0. Next: walk, turn (left, right, up,
           down) or picture. This is a demo; nothing here is yours.

STATUS
       Exists.
