NAME
       groups - shared account places and flat memberships, not friends

DESCRIPTION
       A group is a named place with one owning account and a flat list
       of accepted members. The owner may designate group administrators.
       Group administrators may invite people and remove ordinary members;
       only the owner may promote/demote group administrators. A system
       admin role is not implicit membership or permission to republish.

       GET /api/account/groups lists your memberships. POST with name and
       request_id creates a group. GET /api/account/groups/{id} discloses
       the member identities only to accepted members. No public account
       or group-member directory is introduced.

       POST /api/account/groups/{id}/invite takes a known username and a
       required request_message. It returns an opaque acknowledgment for
       unknown names as well, not evidence of membership or delivery.
       GET /api/account/groups/invitations lists only your pending place
       invitations, without the member roster or inviter account identity.
       POST /api/account/groups/invitations/{id} with accept=true joins;
       accept=false declines. An invitation from a removed/demoted group
       administrator cannot be revived by re-promoting that administrator.

       POST /api/account/groups/{id}/members takes an existing member's
       username, role (member/admin, null to remove), and expected_revision.
       POST /api/account/groups/{id}/leave with {} leaves immediately.
       Read a fresh group revision after conflicting updates. The owner
       cannot leave or transfer ownership through these operations.

       These places are independent of the existing msg_contacts graph.
       Joining never adds a friend, opens private messaging, approves image
       exchange, or grants network/federation authority. The future private
       partnership design requires a request message, mutual consent before
       exposure, and a minimum wait before direct server-to-server activity.
       That future wait is not selected or implemented by this change.

       Each content service resolves its own authoritative owner and then
       uses the shared content policy. This does not replace owned_assets,
       alter a photo's job-owner cancellation rights, or import account
       history from another identity. Personal is the safe default when
       no sharing record exists. Public explicitly means Internet readers.
