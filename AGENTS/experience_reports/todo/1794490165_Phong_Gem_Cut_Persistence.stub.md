# Phong gem cut persistence

The 2026-11-12 Phong gem cutter is a display-only convex marble shell trial. Before using its facets as a saved object or ray-exposure subject, establish the cut shell's interior material domains, optical boundaries, mass and contact contract. Then evaluate arbitrary convex source solids. See `../1794490165_DOC_Phong_Gem_Cut_Trials.md`.
